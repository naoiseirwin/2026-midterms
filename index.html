How this works

We simulate the election 100,000 times. Each simulation starts from the polls (209 Senate polls, 27 national House-ballot polls and 34 presidential approval polls) blended with fundamentals such as how each state usually votes and whether an incumbent is running, then lets states move together the way they do in real elections. Polls are taken from pollsters' and sponsors' own releases, not aggregator or news summaries, and only polls released by 3 Nov are included. Five key Senate races (Alaska, Michigan, New Hampshire, Ohio and Texas) and Alaska's House seat get their own more detailed race models. Betting markets and other forecasters' ratings are never used as inputs; they are shown above for comparison only.
